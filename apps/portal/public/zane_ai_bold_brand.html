The problem

Real estate is a maze of broken handoffs

The industry hasn't changed the way it operates. Buyers are left navigating tools that don't talk to each other, brokers rebuild context from scratch on every lead, and developers have no real-time view of demand. The result: deals take longer, buyers drop off, and everyone loses money to friction.

01

The fragmented buyer journey

A buyer searches on one platform, asks questions via WhatsApp, compares financing on a bank app, and then calls a broker who knows nothing about any of it. Context is lost at every step. Frustration compounds. Deals die.

02

Financing lives outside the conversation

Mortgage estimates, installment plans, bank comparisons — these happen in isolation from the actual property search. Buyers can't make real decisions without understanding what they can afford, in context, right now.

03

Brokers operate blind

Leads arrive with no history. Every client is a cold start. Brokers spend hours rebuilding what a buyer wants, what they've seen, what they can afford — information that was never captured or passed along.

04

Developers can't see demand clearly

Project pipelines live in spreadsheets. Offers get tracked in chats. There's no unified view connecting market interest to actual inventory, pricing decisions, or conversion rates.
