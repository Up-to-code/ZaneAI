The problem

Real estate is
still exhausting.

You open an app. You scroll. You filter. You compare. And after all of that, most of what you see still doesn't feel right.


The tools haven't caught up with how people actually think. Nobody wakes up searching for "3BR, 90sqm, Zone 5." They think about light, and quiet, and their kids' school, and whether they can finally breathe.

"The technology has been forcing people to think like a database. We decided to build something that thinks like a person instead."

The product

Say what you want.
Get what you mean.

Zane-ai is a mobile app for property buyers. Instead of filters and checkboxes, you describe what you're looking for in plain language. The app understands your intent and matches you with properties that actually make sense for your life.


It's not a search engine with a chat box on top. It's a fundamentally different experience — one that puts you at the center.

01

Natural language search

Describe what you want the way you actually think about it. No filters. No technical jargon. Just your words, understood.

02

Intelligent matching

The app reads your intent — not just your keywords — and surfaces the options most likely to feel right for you.

03

Smarter discovery

No endless scrolling. No irrelevant results. A curated, AI-led property experience built around your actual needs.

Built to grow

Our story

Built in Egypt.
Built for the world.

Zane-ai was founded in Egypt — a country with a fast-growing real estate market and a generation of builders who believe the best technology doesn't have to come from somewhere else.

We're an AI-native company. That doesn't mean we put AI on top of an old product. It means AI is in the foundation — in how we search, how we match, how we understand what people actually need.

We're building the MVP carefully and intentionally. Not racing to launch something half-finished. Building the right base, with a clear direction, and a product that earns trust before it asks for it.

We are not building another old real estate interface. We are building the future of property tech.
