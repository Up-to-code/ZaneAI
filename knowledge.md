# Zane AI Product Knowledge

## Who We Are
Zane AI is the intelligent infrastructure for real estate. We build the high-fidelity systems that power how property work actually happens in a modern, connected world.

We are an AI-native company designing a unified intelligence layer for developers, brokers, and buyers.

Our goal is to eliminate the fragmentation of the legacy real estate market by replacing disconnected tools with a single, smart ecosystem that understands intent, automates workflows, and unifies data.

## The Problem We Solve
Real estate buyers often work across fragmented tools, disconnected listings, and incomplete context.
The organization also needs a simple MVP base to manage the product without overbuilding too early.

### Pain Points
- Buyers are forced to scroll generic listings instead of describing what they actually need.
- Search is slow and manual.
- Matching depends on memory, repetition, and follow-up discipline.
- Conversion work gets delayed because the next step is unclear.
- Public explanation and private execution are often split across different tools.
- The organization can overbuild if the MVP boundary is not clear.

### Our Response
Zane AI brings AI-led search, matching, and execution into one connected experience so buyers can act with more confidence and less friction.

## Why Now
The market needs a single intelligence layer now because the cost of fragmentation has become too high.

### Why The Timing Matters
- Buyers expect smarter search, not more static listings.
- AI makes it practical to understand a person’s intent and match it to better options.
- The organization can start lean in an MVP and scale the product only where the codebase proves value.
- Users expect software to reduce manual work instead of adding more of it.
- The market is ready for an intelligence product rather than another disconnected workflow tool.
