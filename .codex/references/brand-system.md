# Zayon Brand System

## Positioning

Zayon is premium, calm, intelligent, minimal, and decision-focused. Every interface should feel like an intelligence layer for real estate, not a noisy listing marketplace.

## Core palette

- Deep Navy: `#0A1428`
- Soft Dark: `#0F1B34`
- AI Red: `#FF3D00`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Secondary Text: `#A8B3CF`
- Muted Text: `#6B7694`
- Card Surface: `#121F3D`
- Divider: `rgba(255,255,255,0.06)`

## Rules

- Use red sparingly: AI signal, send/record button, urgent CTA.
- Prefer deep navy and layered dark surfaces over gradients.
- Keep copy short and active.
- Preserve generous whitespace and strong hierarchy.
- Do not use loud neon AI motifs, glassmorphism noise, or marketplace clutter.

## Typography

- Modern geometric sans serif.
- Premium weight balance: medium for labels, semibold for key decisions.
- Keep body text concise; trim repeated metadata.

## Mobile feel

- Apple-level calm.
- Smooth transitions, minimal chrome.
- Cards should read as decision modules, not feed spam.
